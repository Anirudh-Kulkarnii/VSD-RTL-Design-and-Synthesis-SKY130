# Day 3 — Combinational and Sequential Optimization

## Topics Covered

- Combinational optimization
- Sequential optimization
- Constant propagation
- Redundant logic removal
- Counter optimization
- Hierarchical versus flat synthesis

## Lab Work

The experiments explored how synthesis analyzes RTL functionality and removes unnecessary or redundant hardware.

Different combinational and sequential designs were synthesized and compared to understand the effect of optimization on the resulting circuit.

Counter and multiple-module examples were used to study how hierarchy and synthesis optimization influence the final implementation.

## Tools Used

- Yosys
- SKY130 standard-cell library

## Key Learning

Synthesis is not a simple line-by-line translation of RTL. The synthesis tool analyzes the logic and optimizes the implementation while preserving the intended functionality.
